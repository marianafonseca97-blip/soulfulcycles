FAQ

About the trips

Can I join a trip on my own?

Yes! Most people come on their own. Groups are small, so it's easy to get to know everyone from day one, and you might leave the trip with new lifelong friends :)

How many people go on each trip?

At least 3 and at most 6, so the group is intimate and there's time to talk calmly.

Who books flights and accommodation?

I take care of the booking. Flights and accommodation are paid separately: for Dublin, about €150 for flights and €150 for accommodation.

What do I pay on top of the investment?

Flights, accommodation, food, travel insurance and personal expenses.
