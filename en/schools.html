How I work

Learning through conversation

I believe people learn best by taking part. So instead of lecturing, I use games, group dynamics and plenty of conversation, with materials such as anatomical models, condoms and books.

I try to create a safer space: judgement-free, inclusive and queer-friendly, where every question is welcome, even the ones that feel embarrassing to ask out loud.

FAQ

For teachers and schools

What ages do you work with in schools?

From primary school to university. I adapt each session to the age group and to what the class needs. Before the session, I talk with whoever invites me so everything is tailored.

Can there be more than one session per class?

Yes, and that is what I suggest. It can be a single session, but I recommend a series of sessions with the same class, to explore the topics in more depth.

How much does it cost?

€40 per hour. Each session lasts at least an hour and a half.

Does the school choose the topics?

We decide together. Before the session, I talk with you to understand what the class needs, and students can send the questions and topics they want covered through an anonymous form.
