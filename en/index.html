FAQ

Before you write to me

Are counselling sessions confidential?

Yes. What you share stays between us. Sessions are private and judgement-free, so you can ask anything you feel you need to.

How do online sessions work?

They happen over video call, from home or wherever you feel comfortable. We agree on a day and time by message or email.

What ages do you work with in schools?

I adapt each session to the age group and to what the class needs, from primary and middle school to university. Before the session, I talk with whoever invites me so everything is tailored.

Can I join a trip on my own?

Yes! Most people come on their own. Groups are small, so it's easy to get to know everyone from day one, and you might leave the trip with new lifelong friends :)

How much does a celebration cost?

It depends on what you'd like to include. Tell me what you have in mind and I'll put together a tailored proposal :)

How do I get access to the Recorded Sessions Library?

Subscribe on the library page for €10 per month and I'll share the sessions folder with you on Google Drive within 48 hours. No commitment: cancel anytime.
