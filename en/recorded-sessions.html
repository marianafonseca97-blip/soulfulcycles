Recorded Sessions Library

Three years of conversations about sexuality, feminism and queer topics, to watch at your own pace.

For three years I ran Young Cycles, an online community about sexuality, feminism and queer topics. Every month we had four sessions: a webinar, a debate, a session with a guest and a Q&A session. The sessions were recorded, and you can now watch them anytime, anywhere.
