Sex Education Trainings for Professionals

For youth workers, health and education professionals who want to take these conversations further.

I plan and facilitate trainings with non-formal, participatory methods, in Portuguese or English, for national and international groups.

Example

Building Boundaries and Breaking Taboos

5days of training
17participants
8countries
4workshops created by the participants

I planned and facilitated a 5-day training course on sex education for 17 participants from 8 countries, which resulted in a toolkit so they could take the methods home.

The toolkit gathers the four workshops the participants created and tested, with step-by-step guides for youth workers. It is free to use under a Creative Commons licence.
