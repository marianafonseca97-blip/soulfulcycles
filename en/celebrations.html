Why feminist?

Celebrating who we are

To me, a feminist celebration is a moment to be with your people and celebrate the choices you make and the life you build.

It's also a chance to talk openly about pleasure, bodies and relationships, in a judgment-free space, with time to learn, talk and laugh.

Whether it's a wedding, a birthday or another special date, celebrate with your people, celebrate friendship and celebrate yourself.

FAQ

About the celebrations

How much does a celebration cost?

It depends on what you'd like to include. Tell me what you have in mind and I'll put together a tailored proposal :)

How do I book the date, and what if I need to cancel?

The date is reserved with a deposit of 30% of the proposal. If you cancel more than 15 days ahead, I refund what you've paid, except the deposit. Less than 15 days ahead there's no refund. See the terms and conditions.

Does it have to be a trip?

No. It can be a trip or a single day, afternoon or night.

Who can ask for it?

The person being celebrated or whoever is in charge of organising. If you're not the one organising, send this page to whoever is.

Will you be at the celebration?

If you'd like, yes. Being there during the experience is optional.
