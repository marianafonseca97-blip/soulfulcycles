About me

Hi, I'm Mariana!

Sex educator and founder of Soulful Cycles.

My journey

I started out studying psychology, curious about how people, groups and communities work. Then a podcast episode about menstrual education showed me where I wanted to take it. It made me realise how little most of us were taught about our own bodies, and how much that silence shapes the way we live, love and relate to others.

In 2020 I created Soulful Cycles, the name behind all my work as a sex educator. It comes from the idea of living our menstrual cycles with soul: being truly present and aware of them. Over the years, I opened the project up to sex education in general, an area I have always been interested in. Today, my Master's in Community Psychology is still the base of everything I do: understanding groups, building trust and creating change with people, not just for them. I believe people learn best by taking part, so my sessions are always interactive. Instead of lecturing, I use games, group dynamics and open discussion, and I adapt every session to the people in the room, whether they're 13-year-old students, university students or youth workers from across Europe. The trips I organise grow from the same idea: learning outside the classroom, as a group, with time to explore, ask questions and talk.

Above all, I try to create safer spaces: judgment-free, inclusive and queer-friendly. Today I bring these conversations to schools, universities, organisations and international projects, through workshops, trainings, trips and online content. I hope to keep growing this work, and to see a society where talking openly about sex, bodies and relationships is simply part of growing up.
