Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This website belongs to Mariana Fonseca (Soulful Cycles). I collect as little data as possible and only use it as described here. If you have any questions, write to marianafonseca.97@gmail.com.

Contact form

When you write to me using the form, I receive your name, email, subject and message. I only use this information to reply to you and I don't share it with anyone else. Messages are sent through the Web3Forms service, which forwards them to my email.

Collection waitlist

If you leave your email on the waitlist, I only use it to let you know when collection orders open. You can ask to be removed at any time, just write to me.

Visitor statistics

I use GoatCounter to see how many people visit the site and which pages they view. GoatCounter doesn't use cookies and doesn't store data that identifies you: it only counts visits in aggregate.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies. It only stores the language you chose (PT or EN) in your browser, so it stays the same on your next visit.

Third-party services

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which logs technical visit data (such as IP address) for security reasons. The site's fonts are hosted on the site itself, so there are no connections to Google. Links to Instagram, WhatsApp, Spotify and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time to see, correct or delete the data I have about you by writing to marianafonseca.97@gmail.com. If you think your data hasn't been handled properly, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (cnpd.pt).
