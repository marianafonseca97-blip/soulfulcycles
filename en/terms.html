Counselling

Sessions are paid when you book, by MB WAY, Revolut or bank transfer. You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before the session; if you cancel more than 24 hours ahead, I refund you or we reschedule. Cancellations less than 24 hours before, or no-shows, count as a completed session and are not refunded; in exceptional cases, such as illness, we'll talk.

Package sessions can be used within 6 months of purchase. If you stop partway through a package, I refund the remaining amount, counting the sessions already done at the single-session price (€40).

Trips

Your spot is reserved with a €150 deposit, refunded if the trip doesn't go ahead (for example, if there aren't at least 3 people). If you cancel:

  • up to 60 days before the trip: I refund what you've paid, except the deposit;
  • between 60 and 30 days before: I refund 50% of what you've paid;
  • less than 30 days before: no refund, unless someone takes your spot.

Flights and accommodation follow the cancellation terms of the airline and the accommodation.

Hen parties, birthdays and other celebrations

The date is reserved with a deposit of 30% of the proposal. If you cancel more than 15 days ahead, I refund what you've paid, except the deposit. Less than 15 days ahead there's no refund, because activities and materials are already booked.

Schools, universities and trainings

Dates can be rescheduled with at least 7 days' notice. Other conditions are agreed directly with each school or organisation.

Recorded Sessions Library

The subscription costs €10 per month, paid by card (or Apple Pay / Google Pay) through Stripe, and renews automatically every month on the same date until you cancel. It includes access to the recordings of the Young Cycles community sessions and to an Excel sheet with recommendations on each topic, in a shared Google Drive folder, which I share with you within 48 hours using the Google email you give me. Access is personal: please don't share the folder or the recordings with anyone else.

There's no commitment. You can cancel whenever you like at manage your subscription, using the email you subscribed with. You keep access until the end of the month you've already paid for and won't be charged again; the current month isn't refunded.

As this is digital content, by subscribing you ask for access to start straight away and accept that, once I give you access to the recordings, you can no longer use the 14-day right of withdrawal.
