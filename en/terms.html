What's included

Access to the recordings of the Young Cycles community sessions (webinars, debates, sessions with guests and Q&A sessions) and to an Excel sheet with recommendations on each topic, in a shared Google Drive folder.

Price and payment

The subscription costs €10 per month. Payment is by card (or Apple Pay / Google Pay) through Stripe and renews automatically every month on the same date until you cancel.

Access

After payment, I share the sessions folder with you on Google Drive within 48 hours, using the Google email you give me. Access is personal: please don't share the folder or the recordings with anyone else.

Cancellation

There's no commitment. You can cancel whenever you like at manage your subscription, using the email you subscribed with. You keep access until the end of the month you've already paid for and won't be charged again.

As this is digital content, by subscribing you ask for access to start straight away and accept that, once I give you access to the recordings, you can no longer use the 14-day right of withdrawal. If you have any problem with your access, write to me and we'll sort it out.
